## Consistent usage

- Use **EduOS** as the brand, not a second brand or a separately marketed school-only product. The short headline is the primary brand message; the SEO phrase is keyword positioning, not a second tagline.
- Keep page-specific SEO titles and descriptions distinct for Admissions, Modules, AI, Institution Types and Demo. The homepage hero, social metadata and SoftwareApplication structured data must match their visible product positioning.
- Audience wording is inclusive, not a limitation. Supporting content may cover madrasas, coaching centres, private tutors, polytechnics, LMS-only providers and other institute presets already defined in the product blueprint.
- **AI-assisted tools** describes supported, scoped capabilities; it is not a promise that the full voice assistant, every AI intent or automated write is production-verified. Continue to document actual feature scope, privacy/permission gates and human confirmation accurately.
- Never advertise a fixed number of AI agents, unsupported customer/revenue statistics, or unverified production readiness. Existing technical and dated delivery documents remain historical evidence; do not mechanically rewrite historical architecture or test claims to fit marketing language.
- Apply this positioning to the marketing homepage, relevant landing-page metadata, brand footer, public copy and future promotional materials. Preserve bilingual meaning without forcing an unnatural literal translation.

## Delivery boundaries

Branding changes are text/content only. They do not modify ERP modules, APIs, schema, subscriptions, tenant authorization or deployment schedules. Validate both languages, SEO/canonical/structured metadata and website build output before publishing.
