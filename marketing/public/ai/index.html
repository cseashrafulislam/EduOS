What it helps you do

Illustrative question patterns show how structured filters and approved reports could serve many question variants without generating SQL or fabricating institution figures.

Academic attendance

“How many Class 9 students were absent today?”

Checks permitted class, campus, date and attendance report.

Fees and dues

“Which batch has the highest unpaid fees this month?”

Uses scoped billing/receivable reports rather than generating a number.

HR and employees

“Show staff attendance for the past week.”

Uses approved employee attendance summaries.

Academic results

“Summarize grades for published examinations.”

Restricts data to approved published result records.

Inventory and accounting

“Which inventory items were issued most last month?”

Queries the authorized stock movement/report adapter.

Data and access protection by design

The proposed local-first design uses deterministic bilingual question patterns with optional ML.NET classification, reviewed metric/filter registries, parameterized and permission-scoped ERP reports. Drafted voice actions require review and the existing server-authorized workflow. No fixed agent count is advertised or required.
