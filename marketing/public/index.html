GOOD QUESTIONS, CLEAR ANSWERS

Before you get started.

Your institution is unique. Here are a few useful things to know before requesting a walkthrough.

Is EduOS only for schools?

No. EduOS is designed for multiple education environments, including colleges, universities, madrasas, coaching and training centres. Configuration varies by institution.

Can the interface be used in Bangla?

The platform targets English and Bangla experiences. Individual module and translation coverage should be verified during your demonstration.

Can I see the software before choosing it?

Yes, a live demonstration link can be provided when the demo environment is published. The button on this site will link directly to that environment once configured.

Will every institution use the same settings?

No. EduOS is designed for configurable academic terminology, structure, modules and workflows rather than a fixed institution size or setup.
